Questions parents ask

How is a tutor chosen for my child?

We match on grade, subject, schedule and personality notes from the skills check. If the first match doesn’t feel right, we switch tutors for free.

How much do lessons cost?

Plans start at $19 per lesson on a 12-month plan. See the pricing page for every option.

Which grades do you teach?

Kindergarten through grade 12 for math, and kindergarten through grade 10 for reading and writing.

What does my child need for a lesson?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, headphones and a quiet corner. Lessons run in the browser, so there’s nothing to install.

Will I see progress updates?

Yes. You get a short recap after each lesson and a fuller report every eight lessons.

Can I cancel or pause?

You can pause or cancel from your dashboard at any time. Unused paid lessons stay on your account.
