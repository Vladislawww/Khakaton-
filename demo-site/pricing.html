Pricing questions

How much does a lesson cost?

Between $19 and $26 per lesson for one subject, depending on plan length. Bundling math and reading lowers that by $2 per lesson.

How long is a lesson?

50 minutes, or 30 minutes for kindergarten and grade 1.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, from the dashboard at any time. Unused paid lessons stay on your account for 12 months.

Do you offer refunds?

Unused lessons can be refunded within 30 days of purchase.

Are there sibling discounts?

Yes, a second child gets 15% off any plan.
