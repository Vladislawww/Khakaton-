Reading tutoring FAQ

How do I know if my child is behind in reading?

Signs include guessing words, avoiding reading aloud and trouble retelling a story. Our free check will tell you for sure.

Can you help children with dyslexia?

Yes. Several tutors are trained in structured literacy approaches.

Do you teach writing too?

Yes, from first sentences to five-paragraph essays.

How much does reading tutoring cost?
