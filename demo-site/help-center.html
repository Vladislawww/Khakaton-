Frequently asked questions

Getting started

How do I get started with Lumora?

Book a free lesson, complete the 20-minute skills check, and we’ll match a tutor within 48 hours.

What happens after the free lesson?

Your tutor shares a recommended plan. Choose a subscription to keep the same tutor and schedule.

Is the curriculum aligned with school standards?

Yes, with Common Core and individual state standards.

Lessons & schedule

How do I reschedule a lesson?

Open Schedule in your dashboard, pick the lesson and choose Reschedule. Changes are free up to 24 hours before the start time, and the new time must be at least 24 hours away.

What if my child misses a lesson?

Lessons missed without 4 hours’ notice are counted as used. Two late cancellations per plan period are forgiven automatically.

How do I change tutor?

Go to Profile → Tutor and select Request a new tutor. Switching is always free.

How do I join a lesson?

Five minutes before start time, a Join lesson button appears in the dashboard. Use Chrome, Edge or Safari with camera and microphone allowed.

Billing & plans

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Subscription → Manage plan → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month, and unused lessons remain available.

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes, for up to 3 months at a time, from Subscription → Pause.

How do I upgrade or change my plan?

Choose Subscription → Change plan. The new price applies from the next billing date.

How do refunds work?

Unused lessons can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Contact support to start a refund.

Account & privacy

How do I reset my password?

Click Log in → Forgot password and follow the email link. Links expire after one hour.

How do I update my email or phone number?

Edit them in Profile → Contact details.

How is my child’s data protected?

See our privacy policy. We never sell personal data.

Technical help

The lesson room won’t load

Refresh the page, then check that camera and microphone access are allowed. If it still fails, try another browser or contact support.

My child can’t hear the tutor

Check the headphones are selected as the audio output in the lesson room settings (gear icon).

Which devices are supported?

Windows or Mac laptops, Chromebooks and iPads from the last five years.
