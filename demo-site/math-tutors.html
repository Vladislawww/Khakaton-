Math tutoring FAQ

Does online math tutoring really work?

Yes, when it’s 1:1 and consistent. Most families see clearer understanding within four to six weeks.

How long is each lesson?

Lessons are 50 minutes. Younger kids can choose 30-minute sessions instead.

Do you help with school homework?

Yes. Share the worksheet in the dashboard and the tutor will fold it into the lesson.

Which math subjects are covered?

Everything from counting to calculus, plus statistics and test prep.
