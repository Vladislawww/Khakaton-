1. What we collect

Account details you give us (parent name, email, phone), your child’s first name and grade, lesson schedules, lesson notes written by tutors, and payment records handled by our payment processor.

We also collect basic technical data, such as browser type and connection quality, to keep lessons running smoothly.

2. How we use it

  • To match tutors and run lessons
  • To send schedules, recaps and progress reports
  • To answer support requests
  • To improve our teaching methods using aggregated, de-identified data

3. Who we share it with

Only with the tutor assigned to your child and with service providers that help us operate (hosting, payments, email). We never sell personal data.

4. Children’s privacy

Accounts are created and managed by parents or guardians. We collect only the minimum information about children needed to teach them.

5. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 90 days of closure, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data at any time from Profile → Privacy or by contacting us.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in. Optional analytics cookies are only set with your consent.

8. Terms of service

Using Lumora means agreeing to our terms: lessons are for personal, non-commercial use, and subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled.
