# Billing and subscription

How to manage, pause, change, cancel and get refunds for a Lumora subscription. All of this lives in the parent dashboard under **Subscription**.

## Where to manage my plan
Open the parent dashboard and go to **Subscription → Manage plan**. There you can see your current plan, the number of lessons left this month, the next billing date, and buttons to change, pause or cancel the plan.
- URL: /dashboard/subscription
- Tools: show_subscription

## How billing works
Subscriptions are billed monthly, on the same day of the month as the day the plan started. The bill covers the lessons for the coming month. Subscriptions renew every month until cancelled. Invoices and receipts are under **Subscription → Payments**. Lumora accepts major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Card details are handled by our payment processor; Lumora never sees or stores the full card number.
- URL: /dashboard/subscription/payments
- Tools: show_payments

## How do I upgrade or change my plan?
Choose **Subscription → Change plan**. You can switch to a longer or shorter plan, change the number of lessons per week, or add or remove a subject (for example, move from math only to the math + reading bundle). The new price applies from the next billing date. Your tutor and time slots stay the same.
- URL: /dashboard/subscription
- Tools: change_plan

## Can I pause my subscription?
Yes. Go to **Subscription → Pause**. You can pause for up to 3 months at a time. During a pause you are not billed and no lessons are scheduled. Your tutor is kept for you, and unused lessons stay on your account. The subscription resumes automatically on the date you pick, or you can resume earlier from the same page. The 12-month plan includes a summer pause.
- URL: /dashboard/subscription
- Tools: pause_subscription, resume_subscription

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to **Subscription → Manage plan → Cancel**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month, so you can still use the lessons you have already paid for. Unused paid lessons stay on your account for 12 months and can be booked any time. You can cancel from the dashboard at any time; there is no phone call needed.

Before cancelling, consider pausing instead (up to 3 months) or switching tutor for free, if the reason is a break or a tutor mismatch.
- URL: /dashboard/subscription
- Tools: cancel_subscription

## How do refunds work?
Unused lessons can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Lessons that were already taken, or counted as used because of a late cancellation, are not refundable. To start a refund, contact support through the chat; support checks which lessons are eligible and confirms the amount before processing. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
- URL: /help-center.html#billing
- Tools: request_refund

## What happens to unused lessons?
Unused paid lessons stay on your account for 12 months from purchase, including after a pause or cancellation. You can book them with your tutor any time in that period.

## My payment failed
If a monthly payment fails, Lumora retries the card automatically over the next few days and emails you. Lessons that are already scheduled are kept during that time. To fix it, update your payment method under **Subscription → Payments → Payment method**. If the problem continues, contact support.
- URL: /dashboard/subscription/payments
- Tools: show_payments, update_payment_method

## I was charged twice or don't recognize a charge
Open **Subscription → Payments** and check the invoices. A charge can look duplicated if a failed payment was retried; usually only one goes through and the other disappears within a few days. If you still see two completed charges for the same month, contact support with the dates and amounts and we will investigate and refund the duplicate.
- URL: /dashboard/subscription/payments
- Tools: show_payments, request_refund
