# Plans and pricing

Lumora has three subscription plans. They differ in length and price per lesson. Every plan includes a free first lesson, progress reports, homework help and free tutor switches.

## Plan prices
Prices are per lesson, billed monthly. The full price without a plan would be $29 per lesson.

| Plan | One subject | Math + reading bundle | Saving |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 months | $26 per lesson | $24 per lesson | save 10% |
| 6 months (most popular) | $23 per lesson | $21 per lesson | save 20% |
| 12 months | $19 per lesson | $17 per lesson | save 35% |

Bundling math and reading lowers the price by $2 per lesson. The monthly bill is the price per lesson multiplied by the number of lessons per week you chose (one, two or three) for that month.

Example: a 6-month plan, one subject, two lessons a week, is about 8–9 lessons a month at $23 each.

## What each plan includes
- **3 months — best for a quick boost:** skills check and plan, 2 progress reports, homework help.
- **6 months — steady, visible progress:** everything in 3 months, 4 progress reports, practice buddy access.
- **12 months — lowest price per lesson:** everything in 6 months, a monthly parent call with the tutor, and a summer pause included.

## What every plan includes
- A free first lesson, with no card needed.
- Progress recaps after every lesson and reports every eight lessons.
- Homework help.
- Free tutor switch at any time.
- Support chat seven days a week.
- No sign-up fee.
- Flexibility: pause, switch subjects or change tutor whenever you need.

## How much does a lesson cost?
Between $19 and $26 per lesson for one subject, depending on plan length. Bundling math and reading lowers that by $2 per lesson ($17–$24). Lesson length (30 or 50 minutes) does not change the price.

## Are there sibling discounts?
Yes. A second child in the same family gets 15% off any plan. The discount is applied to the second child's subscription. Ask support to link the siblings' accounts if the discount does not appear.

## Which plan should I choose?
Not sure? Start with a free lesson. The tutor will recommend a pace after the first session.
- For a short goal (a test next month, catching up on one topic): 3 months.
- For steady progress over a school year: 6 months.
- For the lowest price and long-term support: 12 months, which also includes a summer pause.

## Do prices include tax?
Prices on the pricing page are shown before any local sales tax. If tax applies in your state, it is shown on the invoice.
