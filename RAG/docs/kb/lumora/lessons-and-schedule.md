# Lessons and schedule

Everything about booking, moving, joining and missing Lumora lessons. All schedule actions live in the parent dashboard under **Schedule**.

## Where to see the lesson schedule
Open the parent dashboard and go to **Schedule**. It lists every upcoming lesson with the date, time (in your local time zone), subject and tutor. Past lessons show the tutor's recap.
- URL: /dashboard/schedule
- Tools: show_schedule

## How do I reschedule a lesson?
Open **Schedule** in the dashboard, pick the lesson and choose **Reschedule**, then pick a new free slot with the same tutor. Rescheduling is free up to 4 hours before the lesson starts. Within 4 hours of the start, the lesson can no longer be moved; it can only be cancelled as a late cancellation (see below).

Tips: evening slots fill fast, so book early. Weekend slots are available.
- URL: /dashboard/schedule
- Tools: reschedule_lesson

## How do I cancel a single lesson?
Open **Schedule**, pick the lesson and choose **Cancel lesson**. If you cancel at least 4 hours before the start time, the lesson is returned to your balance and you can book it again later. Cancelling one lesson does not cancel the subscription. To stop the whole subscription, see "How do I cancel my subscription?" in Billing and subscription.
- URL: /dashboard/schedule
- Tools: cancel_lesson

## What if my child misses a lesson or I cancel late?
Lessons missed or cancelled with less than 4 hours' notice are counted as used. To be fair to families, the first **two late cancellations per plan period are forgiven automatically**: the lesson is returned to your balance without you doing anything. From the third late cancellation in the same plan period, the lesson counts as used.

If the tutor misses or cancels a lesson, the lesson is always returned to your balance and you get priority when rebooking.

## How do I join a lesson?
Five minutes before the start time, a **Join lesson** button appears on the dashboard home and in **Schedule**. Click it to enter the lesson room. Use Chrome, Edge or Safari and allow camera and microphone access when the browser asks. If the room doesn't load, see Technical help.
- URL: /dashboard
- Tools: join_lesson

## What a lesson looks like
Each lesson is live and 1:1 on a shared interactive whiteboard. A typical 50-minute lesson has a quick warm-up game, the main topic, practice problems, and a two-minute "what I learned" recap where the child explains the idea back. Lessons for children with ADHD or learning differences use shorter segments, movement breaks and visual tools.

## How many lessons a week?
You choose: one, two or three lessons a week. Your tutor recommends a pace after the free lesson. Most families of younger children choose two lessons a week. You can change the pace later by changing your plan.

## Progress recaps and reports
- After **every lesson** you get a short recap (what was covered, how it went, what to practice).
- After **every eight lessons** you get a fuller progress report with skills mastered and the plan for the next weeks.
- 12-month plans also include a **monthly call** with the tutor.
Recaps and reports are in the dashboard under **Progress** and are also emailed to you.
- URL: /dashboard/progress
- Tools: open_link

## Homework help
Every plan includes homework help. Upload a photo or file of the school worksheet in the dashboard before the lesson, and the tutor will fold it into the session.

## Practice buddy between lessons
Practice buddy is an AI helper that suggests short exercises between lessons, based on what was covered. It is included in 6-month and 12-month plans. It never replaces the tutor and does not do homework for the child.
