# Tutors

How Lumora selects tutors, how a tutor is matched to your child, and how to change tutor.

## How tutors are selected
Every Lumora tutor passes a subject test, a mock lesson and a background check. Fewer than one in nine applicants are hired. New tutors are trained in the Lumora lesson method and observed before their first student.

## How is a tutor chosen for my child?
Lumora matches on grade, subject, schedule and the personality notes from the free skills check. The match usually takes up to 48 hours after the free lesson booking. Your child keeps the same tutor every week, so trust and momentum build naturally.

## How do I change tutor?
Go to **Profile → Tutor** and select **Request a new tutor**. Switching is always free, on every plan, as many times as you need. You can add a short note about what you'd like to be different (for example, "calmer pace" or "more evening slots"). Lumora proposes a new tutor, usually within 48 hours, and your future lessons move to the new tutor once you confirm. Your lesson balance and plan stay the same.
- URL: /dashboard/profile/tutor
- Tools: change_tutor

## Support for learning differences
Tutors adapt lessons for children with ADHD, dyscalculia or dyslexia: shorter segments, movement breaks, visual tools, and for reading, structured and multisensory literacy approaches at a gentle pace. Mention this in the skills check or in **Profile → Tutor** notes so we match a tutor with that experience.

## Can I choose a specific tutor?
Yes, if they have free slots that match your schedule. Click **Book a trial** on the tutor's card on the math or reading tutors page, or tell support which tutor you'd like.
