# Known issues (internal)

Current bugs and workarounds. Customers can be told there is a known problem and the workaround, but not the internal details or dates.

## Apple Pay payments failing for some families
Since the October release, some Apple Pay renewals fail with a generic error. Workaround: ask the parent to add a card or use Google Pay under Subscription → Payments → Payment method. Engineering ticket BILL-412. Group all reports into the "Apple Pay failure" cluster in the issue bank.

## Safari 17 on older iPads: no tutor audio
On iPads with Safari 17, tutor audio sometimes doesn't play after joining. Workaround: rejoin the lesson, or update iPadOS. Ticket ROOM-219.

## Reschedule shows no free slots on Sundays
The reschedule picker sometimes hides Sunday slots for tutors in another time zone. Workaround: support can book the Sunday slot manually. Ticket SCHED-88.
