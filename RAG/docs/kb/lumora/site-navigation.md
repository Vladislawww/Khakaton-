# Where to find things: site and dashboard map

Use this to direct parents to the right page or dashboard section.

## Public website pages
- Home, with the grade picker to book a free lesson: /index.html
- Math tutors: /math-tutors.html
- Reading tutors: /reading-tutors.html
- Grade 1 math: /grade-1-math.html
- Parent reviews: /reviews.html
- Pricing and plans: /pricing.html
- Help center with searchable FAQ: /help-center.html
- Free worksheets: /worksheets.html
- Blog: /blog.html
- About Lumora and open jobs: /about.html
- Privacy policy and terms: /privacy.html
- Tools: open_link

## Parent dashboard: Schedule
Upcoming and past lessons, the Join lesson button, rescheduling and cancelling single lessons.
- URL: /dashboard/schedule
- Tools: show_schedule, reschedule_lesson, cancel_lesson, join_lesson

## Parent dashboard: Subscription
Current plan, lessons left, next billing date, change plan, pause, resume and cancel. **Payments** inside it shows invoices, receipts and the payment method.
- URL: /dashboard/subscription
- Tools: show_subscription, change_plan, pause_subscription, resume_subscription, cancel_subscription, show_payments

## Parent dashboard: Profile
- **Contact details:** email and phone number.
- **Tutor:** current tutor and "Request a new tutor".
- **Children:** add a child or edit a child's grade.
- **Privacy:** export or delete data.
- **Time zone:** used for all lesson times.
- URL: /dashboard/profile
- Tools: show_account, update_contact_details, change_tutor

## Parent dashboard: Progress
Lesson recaps after each lesson and progress reports every eight lessons.
- URL: /dashboard/progress
- Tools: open_link

## Signing in
Click **Log in** in the top right corner of any page. Use **Forgot password** to get a reset link by email.
- URL: /login
- Tools: reset_password
