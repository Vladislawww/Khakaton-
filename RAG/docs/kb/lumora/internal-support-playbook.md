# Support playbook (internal)

For the Lumora support team and the AI agent's operators. Never quote this to customers.

## Tone and principles
Be warm, short and concrete. Solve first, explain second. Never blame the parent or the tutor. Always confirm a state-changing action (reschedule, cancel, pause, plan change, refund) with the parent before doing it.

## Escalate to a human when
- The parent asks about a refund above $150 or outside the 30-day window.
- A safeguarding concern about a child or a tutor is mentioned (escalate immediately, priority P1).
- A complaint about a tutor's behavior (not just fit).
- Legal threats, chargebacks, or requests from a lawyer or the press.
- Three clarification rounds have not resolved the issue.
- Data deletion or export requests that the parent cannot complete in Profile → Privacy.

## Refund rules beyond the public policy
Agents may approve refunds for unused lessons within 30 days up to $150 without approval. Goodwill credit of one free lesson may be given once per family per 6 months for a lesson lost due to a Lumora-side technical failure. Anything else goes to the billing lead.

## Late cancellation exceptions
Beyond the automatic two forgiven late cancellations per plan period, an agent may forgive one more for illness or a family emergency, once per plan period. Note the reason on the ticket.

## Response time targets
Chat first reply under 2 minutes; email first reply under 4 hours; P1 safeguarding under 15 minutes, 24/7.
