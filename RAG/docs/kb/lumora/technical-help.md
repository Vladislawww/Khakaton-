# Technical help

Fixes for problems with the lesson room, audio, video and devices.

## Which devices and browsers are supported?
- Devices: Windows or Mac laptops, Chromebooks, and iPads from the last five years.
- Browsers: up-to-date Chrome, Edge or Safari.
- Phones work in an emergency, but the screen is too small for the whiteboard, so we don't recommend them.
- Internet: a stable connection of at least 5 Mbps. Close video streaming on other devices during the lesson if the connection is weak.
Lessons run in the browser; there is nothing to install.

## The lesson room won't load
1. Refresh the page.
2. Check that camera and microphone access are allowed for the site (click the lock icon next to the address bar).
3. Close other apps that may use the camera, like video-call apps.
4. Try another supported browser (Chrome, Edge or Safari).
5. If it still fails, contact support through the chat; tell us the device and browser. If a lesson is lost because of a problem on our side, it is returned to your balance.

## My child can't hear the tutor
Check that the headphones are selected as the audio output in the lesson room settings (the gear icon). Also check that the device volume is up and the browser tab is not muted. Bluetooth headphones sometimes connect to a different device; try wired headphones or reconnect them.

## The tutor can't hear or see my child
In the lesson room settings (gear icon), choose the correct microphone and camera. Make sure the browser has permission to use them (lock icon next to the address bar) and no other app is using the camera. On a Mac, also check **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera / Microphone** for the browser.

## The video is freezing or lagging
Move closer to the Wi-Fi router or use a cable, pause downloads and streaming on other devices, and close extra browser tabs. If the video stays poor, the tutor can switch to audio and whiteboard only so the lesson continues.

## The whiteboard isn't responding
Refresh the page; the whiteboard keeps its content. On iPads, make sure Safari is up to date. A stylus makes drawing much easier but is not required.

## The "Join lesson" button doesn't appear
The button appears five minutes before the start time. Check that you are looking at the right child's schedule and that the time zone in **Profile** is correct. If the lesson is starting and the button is still missing, contact support immediately.
- URL: /dashboard
- Tools: join_lesson, show_schedule
