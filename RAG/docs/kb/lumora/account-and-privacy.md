# Account and privacy

Managing the parent account, sign-in, contact details and how Lumora protects family data.

## Who owns the account?
Accounts are created and managed by a parent or guardian. Children do not have their own login; they join lessons from the parent dashboard. One parent account can hold several children, each with their own tutor, plan and schedule.

## How do I reset my password?
Click **Log in → Forgot password**, enter the account email and follow the link we send you. The link expires after one hour. If no email arrives within a few minutes, check the spam folder, then request a new link. Support can also send a reset link to the email on file, but support will never ask for or see your password.
- URL: /login
- Tools: reset_password

## How do I update my email or phone number?
Edit them in **Profile → Contact details**. A change of email is confirmed by a link sent to the new address. Lesson reminders and recaps go to the new contact details from then on.
- URL: /dashboard/profile/contact
- Tools: update_contact_details

## How do I add another child?
Go to **Profile → Children → Add child**, enter the child's first name and grade, and book a free lesson for them. A second child gets 15% off any plan.
- URL: /dashboard/profile/children
- Tools: open_link

## What data does Lumora collect?
Account details you give us (parent name, email, phone), your child's first name and grade, lesson schedules, lesson notes written by tutors, and payment records handled by our payment processor. We also collect basic technical data, such as browser type and connection quality, to keep lessons running smoothly. We collect only the minimum information about children needed to teach them.

## How is my child's data used and shared?
Data is used to match tutors and run lessons, to send schedules, recaps and progress reports, to answer support requests, and to improve teaching methods using aggregated, de-identified data. It is shared only with the tutor assigned to your child and with service providers that help us operate (hosting, payments, email). Lumora never sells personal data.
- URL: /privacy.html

## Can I see, export or delete my data?
Yes. You can access, correct, export or delete your data at any time from **Profile → Privacy**, or by contacting us. Account data is kept while the account is active and deleted within 90 days of closing the account, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
- URL: /dashboard/profile/privacy
- Tools: open_link

## Cookies
Lumora uses essential cookies to keep you signed in. Optional analytics cookies are only set with your consent.

## Terms of service in short
Lessons are for personal, non-commercial use. Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. The full privacy policy and terms are on the privacy page.
- URL: /privacy.html
