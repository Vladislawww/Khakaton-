# Contacting Lumora support

## How to reach support
- **Chat:** "Chat with support" on the help center page and in the dashboard. Support is available seven days a week and usually replies within minutes.
- **Email:** hello@lumora.example (general questions). Partnerships: partners@lumora.example. Privacy questions: privacy@lumora.example.
- There is no phone line; every action (pause, cancel, reschedule) can be done in the dashboard or through the chat.
- URL: /help-center.html
- Tools: open_link

## What support can do for you
Support can explain plans and policies, reschedule or cancel lessons, pause, change or cancel a subscription, request a new tutor, check payments, start a refund for eligible lessons, send a password reset link, and fix technical problems with the lesson room. For any change to your plan or lessons, support shows you the change first and asks you to confirm.

## What support will never ask for
Lumora support will never ask for your password, your full card number or the security code on your card. If someone asks for these in Lumora's name, do not share them and tell us.

## Is Lumora a real company?
No. Lumora Learning is a fictional brand built for a hackathon demo of an AI customer-support agent. Names, numbers, reviews and email addresses on the site are placeholders.
